Differentiation

  • Buyers: Part D / MA-PD sponsors & PBM formulary teams first; biosimilar market access second.
  • Monetize: Free → Pro seats → Enterprise feeds, territory packs, plan-gap module.
  • True rival: “Analyst + Excel on the CMS download” — we win on speed, matching, and packaging.
